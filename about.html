Who's behind the noise

About

Phoenix Phamous is an independent booker putting on loud nights at one room: The Blooze Bar.

The booking

Phoenix Phamous started as a way to keep hard rock and metal on a Phoenix stage on a regular schedule. We handle the whole night end to end — building the bill, making the poster, promoting the show, running the door, and keeping set times tight.

Bills lean toward guitar-driven rock, metal, doom, punk, and tribute acts, plus the occasional variety night like the Blooze Bros Gong Show. Local openers are welcome and touring acts routing through Arizona can pitch a date.

The room

The Blooze Bar is a neighborhood rock bar in north Phoenix with a stage, a real PA, and a crowd that shows up for live music. Shared backline, cash bar, 21 and over.

Want to play it? Send a pitch. Want a table for a show? Email info@phoenixphamous.com.
