Phoenix Phamous books rock, metal, punk, and tribute acts into The Blooze Bar. Fill this out and it opens an email to our booking team with everything in place.

Check these fields

    Spotify, Bandcamp, YouTube, or a press kit page.

    Opens your email app · or write booking@phoenixphamous.com

    What happens next

    1. We listen

      Every submission gets played. We reply within about two weeks, booked or not.

    2. We slot the date

      If it's a fit, we offer a date and a bill, and confirm set length and load-in.

    3. We promote it

      Custom poster art, socials, and mailing list. You get assets to share too.

    4. You play the Blooze

      Backline is shared, doors at 7, sound handled. Split is discussed up front.
